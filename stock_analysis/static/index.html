Five pillars

A note stays blank until a source fills it.

This desk renders the equity framework and marks every feed-backed figure as data not connected. It does not call a model and it does not invent a price, a flow, or an earnings date.

  1. Fundamental health. Revenue, profitability, leverage, valuation, and catalysts.
  2. Price trend and volume. Trend, support, and resistance only when bars were supplied.
  3. Buying versus selling pressure. 13F, Form 4, and short interest in the US. Shareholding, FII/DII, bulk and block deals, and exchange short or SLB in India.
  4. Alignment. Confirmation or divergence only when both a trend and a flow series exist.
  5. Summary. Bullish factors, bearish risks, and what to watch. Each bullet cites a fact or says it is missing.
